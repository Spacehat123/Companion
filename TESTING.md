## 📲 How to Install (Feels Best as Fullscreen App)

* **iPhone / iOS Safari**:
  1. Open the link above in Safari.
  2. Tap the **Share** button at the bottom (square with arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
  4. Launch the **Kitsune** icon from your home screen.
* **Android Chrome**:
  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the **three dots menu** (top-right) $\to$ **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
  3. Launch from your apps.

## 🐾 How to Play

Just interact with it directly like a real creature:
* **Tap it**: Poke its head, tummy, ears, or tail (each feels different!).
* **Hold it**: Pet it gently to see how it purrs and leans into your touch.
* **Stroke it**: Drag your finger across it to give continuous cuddles.
* **Flick it**: A quick swipe will fling it across the screen.
* **Shake your phone**: Casual shake triggers its dizzy wobble and controls morph.
* **Leave it alone**: Watch what it does spontaneously when ignored!

When the small question at the bottom asks **"Did that make you smile?"**, just tap **Yes** or **Not really**.
